# One-Verb Cinema + Loyalty Compass

**Goal:** Each role understands *why they are playing* and *whom to support* in five seconds. One verb per 30-second round. Crises play as cinema, not cards.

**Approved:** Approach A. Candidate desk ≠ committee-head desk. Each head’s desk is department-themed.

## Always visible

- **Head:** icon, department room name, one-line WANT, compass (Builder vs Star: LEAN / TOSS-UP).
- **Candidate:** Campaign HQ, “need 3 of 5 votes”, five department faces (Allied / Listening / Cold).
- Clock. Incoming deal as a full-screen interrupt (accept / reject only).

Heads never see the other four heads.

## One verb

- Manifesto: candidate slaps two icons on a poster. Head watches compass and may sell a vote.
- Crisis: full-bleed cinema. Candidate picks 2 doors (not 3). Head watches, then stamps Helps me / Burns me.
- Deal: 3 prices (min / min+10 / ₹40). Project auto-fills that head’s flagship. Currency is the vote. Public backing is a separate stamp.
- Vote: tap one face.

## Cinema

Tabloid headline, one sting line, two impact icons, ken-burns grade. Feed stays hidden except verdict slams.

## Client-only first

Same reducers and tables. Hide options in the UI. Drop the third crisis option from the screen (map doors to optionIndex 0 and 1, or 0 and 2 when that pair is the real conflict).
